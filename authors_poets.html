Authors/Poets

Robert Frost

Robert Frost (1874–1963) was an American poet known for his depictions of rural life and his mastery of colloquial speech. His poetry often explored themes of nature, human experience, and philosophical reflection. He won four Pulitzer Prizes for Poetry and remains one of the most celebrated poets in American literature.

Famous Poems by Robert Frost

  • The Road Not Taken
  • Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening
  • Mending Wall

William Blake

William Blake (1757–1827) was an English poet, painter, and printmaker known for his visionary and mystical works. His poetry often explored themes of spirituality, imagination, innocence, and experience, and he is considered one of the key figures of Romanticism.

Famous Poems by William Blake

  • The Lamb (innocence)
  • The Tyger (experience)

More Info



Emily Dickinson

Emily Dickinson (1830–1886) was an American poet known for her unique style, short lines, and unconventional punctuation. Her poetry often explored themes of death, immortality, nature, and personal identity. Though she lived much of her life in isolation, her work has become some of the most celebrated in American literature.

Famous Poems by Emily Dickinson

  • Because I Could Not Stop for Death
  • Hope is the Thing with Feathers
  • I’m Nobody! Who Are You?

More Info



Langston Hughes

Langston Hughes (1902–1967) was an American poet, social activist, and playwright, best known as a leading figure of the Harlem Renaissance. His poetry focused on the struggles and beauty of African American life, using jazz rhythms and everyday speech.

Famous Poems by Langston Hughes

  • Harlem (Dream Deferred)
  • The Negro Speaks of Rivers
  • I, Too




Sylvia Plath

Sylvia Plath (1932–1963) was an American poet and novelist known for her confessional poetry. Her work often explored themes of mental illness, self-identity, and female oppression. She won a posthumous Pulitzer Prize for her poetry collection *The Collected Poems*.

Famous Poems by Sylvia Plath

  • Daddy
  • Lady Lazarus
  • Ariel
