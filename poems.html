Moonlit Serenade

The silver moon in midnight's grace,

Watches over time and space.

Her silent glow, so soft, so bright,

Bathes the world in peaceful light.

Stars above in cosmic dance,

Hold us still in mystic trance.

Night unfolds her gentle tune,

Beneath the watchful eye of the moon.


Echoes of Time

Footsteps fade on ancient stone,

Memories carved where time has flown.

Shadows stretch where stories lie,

Beneath the vast and endless sky.

Through echoes deep and whispers old,

The past returns in tales retold.

Each grain of sand, each distant chime,

Speaks softly of the march of time.


Dreamer's Flight

A dream takes flight on wings unseen,

Through fields of gold and skies serene.

It knows no bounds, no walls confine,

A journey endless, pure, divine.

Beyond the stars, beyond the sea,

It soars where hearts and spirits free.

A whisper soft, a beacon bright,

Guiding souls into the light.

Waves of the Ocean

The ocean sings a timeless song,

Where waves embrace and roll along.

It whispers tales of worlds untold,

Of sunken ships and treasures old.

Its rhythm beats against the shore,

A lullaby forevermore.

With salty breeze and endless blue,

It calls to hearts both old and new.


Whispers of the Wind

The wind whispers through the trees,

Carrying secrets on a gentle breeze.

It sings of love, of joy, of pain,

And dances lightly in the rain.

Through silent nights and golden days,

It weaves its song in endless ways.

A poet's muse, a sailor's guide,

Forever flowing, far and wide.


~poetry
